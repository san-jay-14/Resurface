Privacy Policy — Dibs

Last updated: 14 July 2026

Dibs (“we”, “us”, the “app”) is a personal tool that saves content you share into it and resurfaces it at the right moment. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using Dibs you agree to this policy.

Contact: sanjay.codesign@gmail.com

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email address, and profile photo. You may also use Dibs as an anonymous guest, in which case no identifying account information is collected.

Content you save. Links, reels, articles, places, and any notes or “why I saved this” intent you add. This is the core purpose of the app.

Location. With your permission, approximate and precise location are used to show places on a map and to resurface saved places when you are nearby. Location is used only while you are using the app (foreground). We do not track your location in the background.

Photos. With your permission, photos you choose for a profile picture or to attach to a bug report.

Profile details you provide. Your birthday and home city, collected during onboarding to time occasion-based resurfacing.

Device / push tokens. A push notification token so we can deliver resurface reminders, plus basic device information required to operate the app.

How we use your information

  • To save, organise, categorise, and resurface your content at relevant moments.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send notifications you have opted into.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email above. Soft-deleted saves are recoverable for 30 days, then permanently removed.

Security

Data is stored in Supabase and protected by row-level security so that you can only access your own records. Auth tokens are stored in the device secure store.

Children

Dibs is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
